Since 2017

From coffee roots to a neighborhood market.

Candlewood Market says it has been family owned and operated since 2017, with coffee-roasting roots along the shores of Candlewood Lake in Brookfield, Connecticut. The Fairfield storefront brings together coffee, nitro brews, teas, smoothies, fresh organic juices, sandwiches, and a selection of local products.

Its bright, spacious market is designed for more than a quick stop: there are comfortable places to lounge, sturdy tables for getting work done, and Wi-Fi for staying connected.
